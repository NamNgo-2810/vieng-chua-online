**Pitch:** Step into one shared pixel-art pagoda in your browser: walk the courtyard, ring the bell, light incense and chant alongside whoever else is there right now.

**Genre:** cozy social space / virtual place (multiplayer, browser)

**Controls:** WASD or arrow keys (on-screen joystick on mobile) to walk; walk up to a prop and act on it; type to chat.

**Question:** do the people who visit come back tomorrow, and do they do things together while they're there?

**Hooks:** a real, live shared temple — you see other visitors' rituals as they do them; merit you earn adds to a temple everyone tends together.

## First minute
Type a name, enter the temple and appear in the lower courtyard among whoever is online. Walk toward the incense burner, a prompt appears, light incense: smoke thickens, a soft tone plays, your merit ticks up and everyone sees a toast. A chat bubble pops above someone's head: "chào bạn". *(provisional — the place-based actions, merit and chat are not built yet)*

## Challenge
None by design — there is no way to lose. The pull is gentle: today's streak, merit toward the next temple milestone, and a group ritual that needs several people at once. *(provisional)*

## Core loop
- Arrive → walk to a sacred spot (bell, burner, altar, cushion, wooden fish) → perform its ritual.
- Each ritual earns merit (công đức) and is seen by everyone present.
- Shared merit makes the temple more beautiful over time; your daily streak brings you back.
- Chat and join others' rituals while you're there.

## Goal & fail state
- A visit feels complete when you've performed at least one ritual and kept your daily streak; there is no fail state.
